# Known issues and limits

* Only **Flood at Redbank** exists so far; later phases add scenarios, expeditions, progression (see PROGRESS.md).
* Difficulty is untuned for humans: greedy bots with exact previews win ~90% of seeds on both routes (upper bound for humans).
  Tournament-driven tuning is scheduled (phase 11).
* Browsers verified: Chromium (Playwright build 1.63). Firefox and WebKit are not installed in this environment, so they are unverified.
* No real-device testing has been done.
* Art and audio are procedural placeholders of decent quality but not final; see phase 12 notes in PROGRESS.md.
* Human playtests have not been run. No claim about fun beyond automated evidence is made.
